Scenarios

Values follow typical Assam CTC practice: fine plucking of two leaves and a bud, a 12 to 18 hour wither to about 69% moisture, three CTC cuts, 60 to 90 minutes of oxidation at 24 to 27 °C, fluid bed drying to about 3% moisture, and grading into BOP, BP, PF, PD and Dust. Kinetic constants and grade sizes are illustrative, tuned to match published ranges.
